# English-to-Chinese Strategy for Ren'Py

Treat Ren'Py text as interactive dramatic localization.

## Quality order

After structural integrity passes:

1. Preserve plot facts, intent, relationships, clues, and choice consequences.
2. Preserve character voice and scene continuity.
3. Produce idiomatic Simplified Chinese.
4. Fit the text-box, click rhythm, pauses, and any voice performance.

Faithfulness is not word-for-word correspondence. Translate the speech act:
command, evasion, teasing, threat, flirtation, sarcasm, reassurance, or other
intent supported by context.

## Dialogue

- Prefer spoken Chinese appropriate to the relationship and scene.
- Omit repeated English subjects when Chinese naturally permits it.
- Preserve deliberate emphasis, accusation, contrast, and address.
- Keep emotional and vulgar intensity; do not sanitize.
- Avoid unnecessary expansion that slows the scene or overfills the box.

## Narration and internal monologue

- Keep narration distinct from character dialogue.
- Match the project's established literary level rather than making all
  narration ornate.
- Treat internal monologue as its own voice: it may be colloquial thought,
  reflective prose, or an unreliable narrative channel.
- Preserve intentional uncertainty about identity, gender, motive, or referent.

## Menus and UI

- Translate a menu option as the player's intended action or attitude.
- Keep choices concise without erasing meaningful differences between routes.
- Keep UI terminology stable and functional.
- Do not give system UI a character voice unless the project clearly does so.

## Names, terms, and cultural material

- Apply approved names and locked terms consistently.
- Preserve an unapproved proper name instead of inventing a transliteration.
- Localize idioms, humor, and cultural references by function when literal
  wording would fail, while retaining setting and characterization.
- Flag songs, poems, puns, riddles, and culturally dependent jokes for focused
  review.

## Chinese presentation

- Use Simplified Chinese punctuation in natural-language text.
- Preserve ASCII punctuation required by Ren'Py syntax or protected tokens.
- Do not insert manual line breaks solely to imitate English wrapping.
- Avoid gratuitous quotation marks and unnecessary explicit pronouns.
- Prefer concise menu labels and readable dramatic lines over rigid symmetry.

## Review questions

- Would this person plausibly say the Chinese line to this listener now?
- Does the translation preserve what the player learns or chooses?
- Does it preserve ambiguity and emotional force?
- Does it read naturally when joined to adjacent lines and `extend` fragments?
- Does it remain consistent with approved earlier scenes without forcing one
  English phrase to have one context-free Chinese translation?
